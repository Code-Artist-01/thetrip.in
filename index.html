Our Story

Born from a love of the road

We began in 2012 with three friends, one laptop and a simple frustration: travel packages felt impersonal, overpriced and full of fine print.

So we built the opposite. Every itinerary we sell is one we'd happily take our own families on. Every hotel is personally vetted. Every price is what it is — no asterisks.

  • Personally tested itineraries across 60+ destinations
  • Transparent, all-inclusive pricing with zero hidden charges
  • Dedicated trip manager on WhatsApp, 24×7
